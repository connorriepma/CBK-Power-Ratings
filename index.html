# Team Rating Home Court Rating Record Conf ATS Avg vs Line

ATS is each team’s record against my own spread, the number that was showing when the result was first saved. A third number is pushes. Avg vs Line is the average margin above (+) or below (−) that spread. Games against non-D1 or unrated teams, or saved without a spread, are not counted. This grades the model against itself. It is not a betting record.
